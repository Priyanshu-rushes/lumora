A channel built like a storybook you can step into

Lumora started as four sketchbooks and a shared question: what if every fantasy world got to keep its own weather, its own rules of magic, and its own reasons to come back? Four years later, it's a channel — no crossovers, no shared universe, just four worlds that happen to live behind the same glowing screen.

Every episode is written to be watched together, rewatched alone, and argued about at the dinner table.
